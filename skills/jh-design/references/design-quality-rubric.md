# Design Quality Rubric

Score each item from 0 to 2.

- `0`: missing or harmful
- `1`: partly resolved
- `2`: clear and verified

## Product Fit

1. The visual direction matches the real audience and frequency of use.
2. The design serves the task instead of imitating a fashionable reference.
3. No invented business facts, metrics, testimonials, or product capabilities appear.

## Hierarchy And Content

4. The page purpose and next action are clear in the first viewport.
5. Input, action, result, and secondary information have distinct hierarchy.
6. Korean text is readable, naturally wrapped, and not squeezed with negative tracking.

## Interaction And States

7. Labels, required states, validation, focus, and feedback are understandable.
8. Empty, normal, error, and real loading states are handled where relevant.
9. Familiar icons and controls are used for their conventional jobs.

## Responsive Quality

10. The layout works around 390px without horizontal scrolling or overlap.
11. Controls keep stable touch-friendly dimensions and text fits inside them.
12. Dynamic content does not cause incoherent layout shifts.

## Reference Translation

13. Each reference has a named role and a concrete adapted principle.
14. The result does not copy proprietary words, identity, imagery, or an entire layout.
15. Structure, interaction, and visual tone form one coherent direction.

## Brand, Copy, And Media

16. The visual system derives from verified identity, materials, content, or product behavior rather than a pasted logo.
17. The opening tension, promise, proof, and CTA are original, specific, and supported by available evidence.
18. Hero media provenance, mobile crop or asset, poster fallback, and copy-safe area are verified.

## Technical Integrity

19. Core behavior and factual output match the pre-design version.
20. No unnecessary framework, library, API, secret, or personal data was added.
21. The production build succeeds.

Target at least `35/42`, with no zero in Brand, Copy, And Media, Technical Integrity, or Responsive Quality. A visually strong result that changes the business logic or uses unverified media does not pass.
